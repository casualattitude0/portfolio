About Me

I'm Xue He-Sheng — known in the dev community as Casual Attitude. I'm a software engineer focused on test and release automation on Apple platforms: XCTest suites, CI/CD pipelines, and the tooling that keeps fast-moving teams shipping reliable iOS and macOS software.

My path started at the NTUT Programming Club, where I served as extracurricular president, hosted 12+ knowledge-sharing workshops, and partnered with Google to bring its Machine Learning Crash Course to campus. I then led a 6-person team to ship TAT, NTUT's campus app — 10K+ downloads at 4.3★ — and kept it running through years of breaking portal and authentication changes. That's where I learned that shipping is the easy part; keeping software reliable is the real work.

Today at buho Interactive I own test and release automation for cross-platform Apple titles. I run the Xcode → TestFlight → App Store pipeline, hold a stable 60 fps across 300+ releases, keep crash-free sessions at 99%, and collapsed a 1-hour manual build into a single ~5-second chat command. I also built and self-host an internal LLM agent (FastAPI, LangChain, Chroma) guarded by a 40+ contract pytest suite — testing AI systems the same way I test apps.

I care most about the input experience. I built AIKeyboard, a custom iOS keyboard extension (Swift, SwiftUI) with a headless XCTest suite that runs on the Simulator on every build — working within the text-input pipeline, extension lifecycle, and Apple's input-API constraints. I break ambiguous goals into testable contracts, automate the repetitive, debug the hard failures, and make every ship decision rest on evidence.

My Process

Concept

Decompose the feature into testable contracts — map risk, edge cases, and success criteria before code.

Prototype

Build a minimal end-to-end slice and the test that proves it, validating assumptions early.

Collaborate

Work across teams to articulate failures, surface quality risk, and align on what 'shippable' means.

Deploy

Automate builds, tests, and releases — Fastlane, CI, headless XCTest — so shipping is repeatable, not heroic.

Reflect

Triage every failure into a reproducible case and turn it into a regression test for next time.
